# Prompt Manager

A Chrome extension for organizing, managing, and accessing your prompts right in your browser.

## 🚀 Features

-   **Secure & Personal**: Your prompts are stored locally and only accessible to you
-   **Save & Organize**: Store and tag your prompts, never lose a great idea again
-   **Quick Search & Access**: Instantly find the right prompt for the right moment
-   **Sync Across Devices**: Your prompts follow you wherever you go (via Chrome sync)
-   **Dark Mode & Customization**: Sleek interface with customization options (coming soon)
-   **Markdown Support**: Write and preview your prompts using Markdown
-   **Export to CSV**: Easily export your prompts for backup or sharing

## 💡 Why Prompt Manager?

AI tools are only as good as the prompts you give them. Prompt Manager helps you unlock their full potential by:

-   Keeping your best prompts organized and accessible
-   Allowing you to quickly find and reuse effective prompts
-   Providing a secure, private space for your prompt library
-   Enabling seamless sync across your devices

## 🛠️ Installation

1. Visit the Chrome Web Store
2. Search for "Prompt Manager"
3. Click "Add to Chrome"
4. Start organizing your prompts!

## 🔒 Privacy

Your privacy is our top priority. Prompt Manager:

-   Stores all data locally in your browser
-   Uses Chrome's sync storage for cross-device synchronization
-   Does not share your data with any third parties
-   Does not transmit your data to external servers
