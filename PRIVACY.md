# Privacy Policy for Prompt Manager

Last updated: March 2024

## Overview

Prompt Manager is a Chrome extension designed to help you organize and manage your AI prompts. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data and what information we collect.

## Data Collection and Storage

### What We Store

-   Your prompts, including:
    -   Prompt titles
    -   Prompt content
    -   Creation timestamps
    -   Last update timestamps (if applicable)

### How We Store Your Data

-   All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's sync storage
-   Your prompts are synced across your Chrome browsers when you're signed into Chrome (if you have sync enabled)

## Data Usage

### How We Use Your Data

-   To display your saved prompts in the extension
-   To enable search functionality within your prompts
-   To allow you to edit and delete your prompts
-   To enable CSV export of your prompts
-   To provide undo functionality for deleted prompts

### Data Sharing

-   We do not share your data with any third parties
-   Your prompts are only accessible to you through your browser
-   We do not transmit your data to any external servers

## Data Control

### Your Rights

-   You have full control over your prompts
-   You can delete prompts at any time
-   You can export your prompts to CSV format
-   You can edit or modify your prompts as needed

### Data Deletion

-   You can delete individual prompts through the extension interface
-   To completely remove all your data:
    1. Uninstall the extension
    2. Clear your browser's extension data for Prompt Manager

## Security

-   All data is stored locally in your browser
-   We use Chrome's built-in security measures to protect your data
-   No external servers or third-party services are involved in data storage or processing

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document with an updated "Last updated" date.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us through the Chrome Web Store developer page.
